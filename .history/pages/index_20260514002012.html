Who am I?

My journey into technology began with a drive to understand how complex systems and networks function. As a first-year programmer, I’ve focused on building a solid foundation in languages like C# and web development while diving into the intricacies of network administration. This hands-on approach allows me to bridge the gap between back-end infrastructure and creative software solutions. I’m motivated by the challenge of problem-solving and am constantly seeking to expand my technical toolkit as I grow within the field.

What do I do?

  • Coding & Scripting: C#, HTML/CSS, PowerShell scripting, and Git version control.
  • Networking: VLAN configuration, DHCP pool management, Subnetting (VLSM), Static and Dynamic Routing, and Access Control Lists (ACLs).
  • System Administration: Experience with Cisco Packet Tracer for network topology design and IP telephony (VoIP) integration.

My Values

My perspective is shaped by a high-stakes commitment to learning and a "reliability-first" mindset. I view technical challenges through a practical lens, ensuring that every network configuration or line of code is as resilient as it is efficient. By combining academic discipline with a hands-on approach to problem-solving, I bridge the gap between classroom theory and technical execution to deliver stable, user-focused results.
